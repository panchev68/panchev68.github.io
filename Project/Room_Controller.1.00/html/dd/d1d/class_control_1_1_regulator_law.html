Description

Fan-speed and floor-heater decisions of the regulator; not thread-safe, the owner serializes the calls.

The law works on the demand: how far the room is from the setpoint in the direction that needs action, positive while heating is needed in Control::RegulatorLaw::Mode::HOT mode or cooling in Control::RegulatorLaw::Mode::COLD mode. Compared with the legacy AVR regulator it

  • raises the fan speed in proportion to the demand instead of always one step per step interval, and lowers it the same way as the demand falls, with a hysteresis of half the low tolerance so the speed does not chatter at a boundary,
  • lands softly: the fan drops to its lowest speed as the room nears the setpoint, and it stops only beyond the low tolerance,
  • holds every relay at least Control::RegulatorLaw::Parameters::minSwitchInterval, except the jump to full speed in a cold room,
  • switches the floor heater off a little before the setpoint, because the floor keeps giving heat, and holds both its states for Control::RegulatorLaw::Parameters::heaterOnDuration.

enum class Control::RegulatorLaw::Mode : uint8_t

Control law selected by the regulator mode; values match the legacy mode bits.

Enumerator
OFF 0U 

Convector and floor heater forced off.

HOT 1U 

Heating: the fan speed rises and the floor heater runs as the room cools below the setpoint.

COLD 2U 

Cooling: the fan speed rises as the room warms above the setpoint.

BLOCK 3U 

Externally blocked; behaves like Control::RegulatorLaw::Mode::OFF.
