Description

Convector fan-speed and floor-heater regulator.

Compares the current temperature with the setpoint and applies the control law of Control::RegulatorLaw: the fan speed follows the deviation, lands softly at the setpoint and stops beyond the low tolerance, and the floor heater runs with hysteresis and minimum switching times. Evaluation runs every Control::RegulatorLaw::Parameters::evaluationInterval, or on the next Control::Regulator::tick() after Control::Regulator::requestEvaluation().

Thread-safe: setters, getters and Control::Regulator::tick() may be called from different tasks. The owner calls Control::Regulator::tick() with the real elapsed time, for example from its control step; the regulator owns no task.
