Description

Serial link over a UART that is already initialized by CubeMX, with a DMA channel per direction.

A transmit and a receive may run in different tasks; two transmits or two receives must not overlap. Buffers must be in RAM the DMA controller reaches, not in the CCM RAM. The per-UART singleton UART1Port derives from it; nothing deletes a link through a base pointer. The HAL needs USE_HAL_UART_REGISTER_CALLBACKS enabled.
