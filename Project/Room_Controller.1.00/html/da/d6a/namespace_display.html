Display Namespace Reference

Description

The room panel: the legacy serial "Climate Control Display" with three digits, two keys and an SMT160 thermometer.

PanelProtocol holds the frames, SMT160 converts and averages the thermometer readings, PanelKeyboard decodes the keys, PanelScreen draws the screens and runs the service menu, and SerialPanel is the task that polls the panel. The CAN panel (CanProtocol and CanPanelServer, described in CanPanelProtocol.md) is deferred and not used.
