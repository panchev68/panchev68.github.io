Description

Half-duplex RS485 transceiver: transmits a frame with the driver enabled and releases the bus afterwards.

The protocol layers bind transmit() and setDirection() as their transmit and direction callbacks. A singleton: the node has one RS485 bus; configure() binds the link and the direction line.
