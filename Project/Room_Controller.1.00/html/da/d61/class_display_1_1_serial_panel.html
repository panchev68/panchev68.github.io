Display::SerialPanel Class Referencefinal

Polls the serial room panel from its own task. More...

Description

Polls the serial room panel from its own task.

A poll repeats every Config::pollGap after an answer, and every Config::retryDelay after a failed one. After a failed poll the task discards the bytes on the line until it is silent, so the next poll starts on a frame boundary. The legacy firmware restarted the whole controller when the panel stayed silent; here the owner only sees that Snapshot::sequence stops changing and treats the link as lost.

Thread-safe: setOutput() and getInput() may be called from any task.
