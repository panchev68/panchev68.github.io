Description

Position of the installation, used for the sunrise/sunset night window of APP::PowerSupply.

Until a position is known it reports the geographic center of Bulgaria (DEFAULT_LATITUDE, DEFAULT_LONGITUDE). updateFromModem() asks the SIMCom location service for the serving cell; its error of a few kilometres moves sunrise and sunset by seconds only. A position can also be set directly with setPosition(), e.g. from a cloud command.

The position is kept in RAM, so it falls back to the default after a reset until the next modem session.

Thread-safe: the two coordinates are read and written together under a critical section.
