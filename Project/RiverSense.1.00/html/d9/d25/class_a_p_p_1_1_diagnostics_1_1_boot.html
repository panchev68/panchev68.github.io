APP::Diagnostics::Boot Class Referencefinal

Singleton checking every device once at start-up and showing the result on the LEDs. More...

Description

Singleton checking every device once at start-up and showing the result on the LEDs.

run() works before APP::PowerSupply and APP::Monitor own the hardware: both INA219 monitors and the TMP102 on I2C1, the DS18B20 and the A02YYUW on the BSP::PowerSwitches::Channel::SENSORS rail and the A7670E modem (power-up and AT answer). A device that does not answer does not stop the boot; the result goes to Runtime, so the first report already carries it, and indicate() shows it.

The LEDs and the private checks live in Impl in the '.cpp'.

Warning
Task context only, from startup() alone: it drives the devices without the owners' coordination.
