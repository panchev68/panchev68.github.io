Description

Measurement cycle singleton: measures the river level and reports it to the cloud.

Owns the monitor task. Every measurement period the task powers the BSP::PowerSwitches::Channel::SENSORS rail, takes the median distance of the A02YYUW and the DS18B20 temperature, and cuts the rail again. A report (modem power-up, registration, HTTPS POST through APP::Cloud, modem power-down) is sent when the report period has elapsed, or at once when the distance moved by REPORT_DELTA_MM or more since the last successful report.

Adaptive period: in normal mode the node measures every NORMAL_MEASURE_PERIOD and reports every NORMAL_REPORT_PERIOD. When two consecutive measurements show a rapid change (isRapidChange()) the node switches to fast mode (FAST_MEASURE_PERIOD / FAST_REPORT_PERIOD) and stays there until no rapid change was seen for FAST_HOLD. While the accumulator is low (BSP::Accumulator::isLow()) every period is multiplied by LOW_BATTERY_FACTOR.

The distance is measured from the sensor down to the water, so a rising river gives a falling distance. The mounting height is not known on the node, so change detection uses only differences.

Every measurement and modem session updates APP::Diagnostics::Runtime, whose fault mask goes into the report. A failed report is not retried before the next report period, so a missing network does not drain the battery. State, task and helpers live in Impl in 'Monitor.cpp'.
