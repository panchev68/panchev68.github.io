BSP Namespace Reference

Description

Supply switches and drivers of the external devices.

BSP::PowerSwitches powers the sensors and the modem only while they are used. BSP::A02YYUW reads the ultrasonic distance sensor, BSP::DS18B20 binds the shared 1-Wire thermometer driver to its pin, and Lib::GSM::A7670E (bound by BSP::Modem) powers the 4G modem and runs its AT command channel. BSP::SolarPanel and BSP::Accumulator own the INA219 monitors of the charger (with the panel threshold, the charge limits and the published accumulator voltage), and BSP::TMP102 is its temperature sensor.
