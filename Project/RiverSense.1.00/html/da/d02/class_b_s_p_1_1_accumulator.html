Description

Singleton of the 6 V GEL accumulator: its INA219 monitor, charge limits and published voltage.

The INA219 at 0x41 sits on the charger output. It is driven by the task of APP::PowerSupply only; I2C1 is shared, so other tasks read the voltage through getVoltage(), which that task keeps up to date: every MPPT step while charging and every idle poll (measureFromSleep()) while the charger waits for the day or for light.
