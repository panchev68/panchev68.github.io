Description

Date and time management classes for embedded systems.

Provides efficient, allocation-free classes for representing and manipulating dates and times. Includes 'Time', 'Date', and the combined 'DateTime', with validation, comparison, formatting, and compact binary packing for storage or transmission.

There is no dynamic memory allocation and every method is 'noexcept'. 'Date::isValid()' rejects a day that does not exist in the given month/year (e.g. 30 February). 'DateTime::pack()' and 'DateTime::unpack()' fit into 32 bits, and the year field holds only 6 bits, so only years 2000-2063 survive a round trip even though 'Date' accepts years up to 2099.
