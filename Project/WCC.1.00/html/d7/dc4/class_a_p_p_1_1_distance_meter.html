Description

Fill-level measurement module singleton.

Measures every "Impl::MEASURE_PERIOD" (30 min) and once more "Impl::AFTER_DISPOSAL_DELAY" (30 s) after "notifyDisposal()" is called (by "UserInterface", once a door closes), without disturbing the periodic schedule. Each measurement pauses "APP::RFID" discovery, powers the 12 V rail through the ref-counted "APP::Supply12V" and opens USART1 only for that one reading ("Impl::measure()"), then closes both again and resumes "APP::RFID"; the actual exchange is "APP/DistanceMeter/Pga460Polled". A reading that fails "Impl::MAX_FAILURES" times in a row is published as "no distance" instead of a stale value.
