Description

Fill-level measurement module singleton.

Measures every "Impl::MEASURE_PERIOD" (30 min; 4 h while "APP::PowerSupply::ChargeIdle" reports idle) and once more "Impl::AFTER_DISPOSAL_DELAY" (30 s) after "notifyDisposal()"; every measurement restarts the period. Each one pauses "APP::RFID" discovery, powers the 12 V rail through "APP::Supply12V" and opens USART1 only for that reading. "Impl::MAX_FAILURES" failed readings in a row publish "no distance" instead of a stale value.

The state and the task live in an Impl defined in "DistanceMeter.cpp".
