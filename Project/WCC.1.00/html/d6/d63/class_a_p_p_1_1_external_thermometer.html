Description

DS18B20 external thermometer module singleton.

Owns two tasks sharing the one DS18B20 driver through an internal mutex: a slow one that reads the temperature every 5 minutes for the PGA460 speed-of-sound compensation ("getTemperature()"), and a fast one that samples every 15 s and raises the fire alarm when the temperature rises 5 C or more within 30 s ("getIsFireAlarmOn()"). The alarm clears on its own once the rise drops below the threshold; a single failed read keeps the published value, the alarm state and the window unchanged.

The state, the tasks and the private helpers live in an "Impl" defined in "ExternalThermometer.cpp".
