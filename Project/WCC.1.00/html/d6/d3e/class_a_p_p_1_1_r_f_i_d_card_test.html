Description

ST25R100 card type and UID test singleton.

Starts the card discovery of the reader ("BSP::ST25R100::manager()") without the access checks of "RFID": no record read, no signature check, no blocklist. Demo only: every activated card (UID of 4, 7 or 10 bytes) pulses "OUT1" and sounds a beep regardless of its content, just to prove the reader sees it — "OUT1" is the same lock output "UserInterface" drives, so this must never run on a release build, nor alongside "RFID" or "UserInterface". A repeat activation of the same UID within "Impl::DEBOUNCE_WINDOW" is ignored (the reader's faster wake-up timing can see a card held near the antenna several times in a row).

The values are the members of "APP::rfidCardWatch" ("RFIDCardTest.cpp"); each field's own trailing comment documents it. A 7-byte card (NTAG21x) can also be dumped — GET_VERSION, then pages 0..131 if it is an NTAG215 — through "Impl::ENABLE_NTAG215_READ" (off by default: it blocks the discovery task for a while).

Needs "ENABLE_RFID" and "ENABLE_RFID_COMMS_TEST" off, because all of them use the reader.
