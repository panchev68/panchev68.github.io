Thread-safe ring buffer of unacknowledged disposal records. More...

Description

Thread-safe ring buffer of unacknowledged disposal records.

Records are kept in order of creation, and one leaves the buffer only when the ESP32 acknowledges its sequence number. When the buffer is full a new record is refused instead of overwriting an older one, and getLostCount() grows; a caller can check isFull() before even starting a disposal. The contents are lost on reset.

void APP::RecordBuffer::countRejectedCard ( )
noexcept

Counts a disposal refused before it could even be attempted.

A caller such as APP::UserInterface that saw isFull() declines the card instead of opening a door for a push() that would only be refused. Without this call such refusals would not show up in getLostCount() at all.
