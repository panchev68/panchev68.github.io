Description

Board inputs singleton: maps a Channel to its pin and returns the raw pin level.

Channels are the front-panel buttons (BUT1_SW, BUT2_SW) and the magnetic door sensors (SW_INP1, SW_INP2). The active level of a channel (which level means 'pressed' or 'door open') and the debouncing stay with the user.
