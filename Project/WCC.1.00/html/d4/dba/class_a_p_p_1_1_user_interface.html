Description

Front-panel disposal module singleton.

After "RFID" accepts a card, both button LEDs blink for up to "SELECTION_TIMEOUT"; a press selects the amount (BUT1 = 5 l, BUT2 = 20 l) and pulses the matching lock (OUT1 / OUT2) for "LOCK_PULSE". A card is refused while a selection runs, a door is open or "RecordBuffer" is full, so a long communication outage stops new disposals instead of losing old records.

The door sensors (SW_INP1 / SW_INP2) decide the record: "DoorEvent::NOT_OPENED" if the door is not open within "OPEN_CHECK_TIME", otherwise "OPENED". A door still open after "OPEN_GRACE_TIME" queues "LEFT_OPEN" and sounds the buzzer for "ALARM_DURATION"; "CLOSED" follows once it closes. Every record carries its RTC time and goes to the ESP32 through "ComModule".

A service card skips the selection: "requestServiceOpen()" pulses "OUT1", "OUT2" and "OUT3" in turn and queues one record with "DoorEvent::OPENED" and "litres" 0, since "OUT3" has no door sensor to verify it.

The state, the task and the private methods live in an Impl defined in "UserInterface.cpp".
