◆ DeviceFault

enum class APP::Commands::DeviceFault : uint8_t
strong

Bits of the device faults byte of STATUS_RESPONSE: a set bit is a device that did not answer the diagnostics ("Diagnostics::run()" at start-up, refreshed every "Diagnostics::Impl::RUNTIME_CHECK_PERIOD" by "Diagnostics::startRuntimeDiagnostics()").

The byte is 0 when the diagnostics did not run.

Enumerator
EXTERNAL_FLASH 

The W25Q128 flash does not answer.

INA219 of the solar panel (0x40).

ACCUMULATOR_MONITOR 

INA219 of the accumulator (0x41).

TEMPERATURE_SENSOR 

TMP102 (0x48).

THERMOMETER 

DS18B20 does not answer the presence pulse.

NFC_READER 

ST25R100 does not open or has a wrong IC identity.

DISTANCE_SENSOR 

PGA460 does not answer a register read.
