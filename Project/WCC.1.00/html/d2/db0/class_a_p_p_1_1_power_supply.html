Description

Solar charger module singleton.

Owns the charger task. The task brings up the INA219 monitors, the TMP102 and the MPPT controller, then runs the MPPT loop every "MPPT::STEP_PERIOD_MS". A failed bring-up is retried; repeated step failures disable the charger and restart the bring-up.

The charger also pauses itself for the night (see "Impl::isNightTime()" in "PowerSupply.cpp"): once the RTC holds a synced time (the ESP32's "SET_TIME") that falls before sunrise or at/after sunset, computed per day by "Lib::Helper::Sunset" for the site position reported by the ESP32 ("ComModule::getLatitude()"/"getLongitude()", "SET_LOCATION") or, until that is received, the fixed "Impl::FALLBACK_LATITUDE"/"FALLBACK_LONGITUDE" (the geographic center of Bulgaria), it disables the charger, puts both INA219 monitors into their datasheet Power-Down mode (not just stops polling them; the TMP102 is simply left unpolled, it has no comparable low-power mode in this driver) and sleeps until day returns, then re-runs the bring-up. An unsynced clock never counts as night, so the charger keeps running until a sync actually confirms it.

Outside that window, insufficient panel light is handled the same way: a panel voltage too low for the buck to regulate down to the accumulator charge limit ("Impl::MIN_PANEL_VOLTAGE_FOR_CHARGING") also disables the charger and sleeps the INA219 monitors, then every "Impl::PANEL_IDLE_POLL_PERIOD" (5 min) briefly wakes just the solar one for one fresh reading (see "Impl::isPanelLightInsufficient()") instead of running the full MPPT loop, until it recovers.

The state, the task and the private helpers live in an Impl defined in "PowerSupply.cpp".

◆ getIsSensorsOk()

bool APP::PowerSupply::getIsSensorsOk ( ) const
nodiscardnoexcept

Reports whether the two INA219 monitors and the TMP102 are considered healthy right now.

Does not touch the shared I2C bus directly: it is already polled continuously by the MPPT loop, so a second, independent transaction from another task (e.g. a periodic "APP::Diagnostics" re-check) would race it. Meant for exactly that use instead.

Returns
true while the charger is actively stepping or idle for the night/insufficient light; false only while stuck retrying a failed bring-up.
