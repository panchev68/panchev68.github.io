Description

12 V outputs sequence test singleton.

Takes the 12 V booster ("APP::Supply12V", it feeds the BTS724G switches), then drives OUT1, OUT2, OUT3 and OUT4 high for 3 s each, in that order, with no gap, and repeats the round without end; the booster stays on. Check with a multimeter or an LED on the output. "UserInterface" must be disabled while this runs (it drives OUT1 and OUT2 too). The step and the round are kept for inspection with a debugger.
