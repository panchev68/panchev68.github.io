Application Layer

Description

Declares the frame types of the ESP32 link (ComService), the enumerations of their fields and the payload sizes.

The ESP32 is the master: it sends commands and the controller only answers. All multi-byte fields are big-endian. Framing, payload layouts, the acknowledged reading of records and removal requests, power and wake-up of the ESP32, door events and service cards are described in 'doc/Protocol.md'.
