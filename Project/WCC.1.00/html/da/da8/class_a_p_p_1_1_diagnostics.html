Description

Startup hardware check singleton.

Checks flash, both INA219 power monitors, TMP102, DS18B20, ST25R100 and PGA460. A device that does not answer does not stop the boot; it is beeped once (see indicate()) and reported to the ESP32 as a bit of Commands::DeviceFault in 'STATUS_RESPONSE'.

startRuntimeDiagnostics() re-checks every Impl::RUNTIME_CHECK_PERIOD (5 min) after that, without disturbing the modules that now own most of the hardware: the flash is re-probed directly (the only check that is mutex-serialized against concurrent callers), while the INA219/TMP102, the ST25R100 and the PGA460 instead mirror the already-published health of APP::PowerSupply, APP::RFID and APP::DistanceMeter (and the DS18B20 that of APP::ExternalThermometer), so no second, independent transaction races the one already running on that bus. A device whose owning module is disabled (see 'APP::Application::ENABLE_*') simply keeps its last known status instead of being re-evaluated.

The controller normally stays powered continuously, so the one-time run() at start-up only happens again after an actual reset/power cycle; APP::Application::ENABLE_RUNTIME_DIAGNOSTICS (independent of APP::Application::ENABLE_STARTUP_DIAGNOSTICS) is what keeps getFaultMask() an up-to-date, ongoing signal in between.

The state and the private checks live in an Impl defined in 'Diagnostics.cpp'.

nodiscardnoexcept

Safe to call whether or not the owning modules it reads from (APP::PowerSupply, APP::RFID, APP::DistanceMeter, APP::ExternalThermometer) have started yet or are enabled at all; a disabled one just leaves its bits as the last run() or re-check found them.
