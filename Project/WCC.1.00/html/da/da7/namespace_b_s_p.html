Board Support Package

Description

Board I/O singletons and chip drivers.

"Inputs" (BUT1_SW/BUT2_SW, SW_INP1/SW_INP2), "Outputs" (OUT1..OUT4) and "ButtonLeds" (BUT1_LED/BUT2_LED) wrap the board's buttons, door sensors and switched outputs. The chip drivers ("INA219", "TMP102", "DS18B20", "PGA460TPWRQ1", "ST25R100", "W25Q128JVSIQ") are thin board-wiring facades over the corresponding header-only driver in the shared library "X:/Lib/IAR/inc" (pins and port instance only; the driver logic itself lives in the library, not here).
