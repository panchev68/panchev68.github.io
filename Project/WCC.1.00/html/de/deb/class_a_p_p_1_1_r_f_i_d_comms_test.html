Description

ST25R100 SPI link self-test singleton.

Opens the reader like APP::RFID does (BSP::ST25R100::instance() and open(): SPI3, register bring-up with read-back checks), retrying every 2 s until it succeeds. Then every 500 ms it reads the 'IC_IDENTITY' register and counts the reads. A good link gives the same value every time, neither 0x00 nor 0xFF (a floating or shorted MISO reads one of those). APP::RFID must be disabled while this runs (both use the reader). The result, the counters and the identity are kept for inspection with a debugger.

The state and the task live in Impl, defined in 'RFIDCommsTest.cpp'.

Outcome of the last step.

Enumerator
RUNNING 

No step finished yet.

PASSED 

Reader opened and the identity reads back valid.

OPEN_FAILED 

open() failed: SPI3 did not open or a register read-back failed. Check SPI wiring and 5 V.

READ_FAILED 

The 'IC_IDENTITY' read did not complete.

BAD_IDENTITY 

The identity is 0x00 or 0xFF: MISO stuck low or high, reader not powered or in reset.

IDENTITY_CHANGED 

The identity differs from the first valid read: noisy or unreliable link.
