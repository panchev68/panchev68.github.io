Description

MPPT tracking and accumulator charge self-test singleton.

Needs "ENABLE_POWER_SUPPLY" on: it samples the running production charger ("APP::PowerSupply") once a second, in step with the outer MPPT loop ("MPPT::OUTER_LOOP_DIVIDER" * "MPPT::STEP_PERIOD_MS"), and keeps a few derived numbers that a raw Live Watch of "PowerSupply" does not: the peak panel power seen since start (compare to the panel wattage dialled into the bench supply), the panel voltage's min/max over the last "Impl::WINDOW_SAMPLES" (the steady-state tracking ripple around the operating point) and the accumulator charge delivered since start, integrated from the charger current (a sanity check of the INA219 calibration against the bench supply's own Ah meter, if it has one). This test touches no hardware of its own.
