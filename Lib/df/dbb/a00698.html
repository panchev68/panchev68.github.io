Description

Power management component providing low-power mode transitions for FreeRTOS tickless idle.

Coordinates system entry into low-power modes and provides mechanisms to register callbacks that prepare peripherals for sleep and restore their state upon waking up. Deep sleep (Stop mode) is automatically withheld while a debugger is attached ('PowerManager::canEnterDeepSleep()'), since Stop mode can make SWD/Live Watch misread or momentarily lose the connection even with the debug domain kept clocked.
