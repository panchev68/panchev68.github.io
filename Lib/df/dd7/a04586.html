Lib::BSP::RFID::NTAG21x::OriginalitySignaturefinal

Description

Verifies that a card UID carries a genuine NXP originality signature.

Every genuine NTAG21x carries a 32-byte ECDSA (secp128r1) signature of NXP over its 7-byte UID, readable with 'READ_SIG' (0x3C, see NXP AN11350). A card whose signature does not verify against the fixed NXP public key is not a genuine NXP chip, for example a UID-changeable clone. The signed message is the UID itself, not a hash of it.

This does not stop a full emulation of a genuine card (UID, signature and an issuer record all copied); catching that is the issuer's responsibility (a signed record, a blocklist, ...).

Needs micro-ecc configured for 'secp128r1' and reachable as 'ThirdParty/micro-ecc/uECC_config.h' (https://github.com/kmackay/micro-ecc); the header compiles to nothing where that is missing.
