Lib::BSP::Thermometer::DTH::Driver< Type::DTH22 >::Diagnostic

Description

Diagnostic counters and last-frame checksum information.

Tracks reasons for parsing failures and stores the last received frame checksum together with the re-calculated checksum used for validation.
