Lib::HAL::DACPortBase< Derived >

Description

template<typename Derived>
class Lib::HAL::DACPortBase< Derived >

CRTP foundation implementing the lifecycle and conversion policy shared by every transfer mode.

Owns no state of its own, not even the native handle or channel: it reaches both through "derived()->getHandle()" and "derived()->getChannel()". Every mode implements "open()" and "close()" identically ("HAL_DAC_Init" and "HAL_DAC_DeInit") and shares the same "setValue()" conversion-register write, so that policy lives here once.

Template Parameters
DerivedHigh-level concrete driver class owning the native handle and channel.
