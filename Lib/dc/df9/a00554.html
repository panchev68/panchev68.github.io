Description

Sunrise and sunset times for a geographic location and date, after the NOAA solar calculator.

Horizon zenith angles are provided for the official, civil, nautical and astronomical definitions. The solar position follows the NOAA spreadsheet formulas (Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms) and each event is refined by a second pass evaluated at the approximate event time, which keeps the error within about one minute for latitudes below the polar circles.
