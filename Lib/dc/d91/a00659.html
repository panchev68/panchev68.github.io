Description

Declares the 'Lib::HTTP::*' classes: a transport-agnostic HTTP/1.1 client and a wolfSSL-backed TLS transport wrapper, for HTTPS connections to a cloud service over any raw byte-stream link (e.g.

an Ethernet socket or a GSM module's data-mode stream).
