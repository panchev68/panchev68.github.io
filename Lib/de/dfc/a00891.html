Description

NXP NTAG21x tag helpers: originality verification and signed card records.

Provides "OriginalitySignature" (ECDSA secp128r1 verification of the NXP-issued UID signature) and "CardRecord" (a product-signed, ECDSA secp256r1-verified record keyed by an issuer policy), both compiling to nothing unless micro-ecc is on the include path.
