Description

Mathematical filters and estimators for signal processing.

Contains lightweight filters and estimators for noise reduction and state estimation in embedded systems: a moving average (Lib::Math::Filter::MovingAverage), a median filter that rejects spikes (Lib::Math::Filter::Median), an exponential low-pass (Lib::Math::Filter::ExponentialSmoothing) and a discrete Kalman filter (Lib::Math::Filter::Kalman).
