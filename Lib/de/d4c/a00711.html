Description

Board support package: hardware-agnostic device drivers built on top of 'Lib::HAL'.

Contains concrete peripheral devices (displays, EEPROMs, flash memory, RFID readers, sensors, LEDs) and the periodic 'DeviceManager' registration facility shared by them.
