State Lib::Digi::API::Connection::Quality::update ( )
inline

Recompute the quality percentage and label from the owning Connection's current Info.

Accumulates penalties for retransmissions, elevated transmit period and low RSSI, decaying them over time in RUNTIME mode, then derives a percentage and label from the combined penalty.

Returns
The newly-computed quality State.
