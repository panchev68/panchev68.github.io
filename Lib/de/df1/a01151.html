Lib::BSP::RFID::NfcManager< T_MODE >::Diagnostics

Description

template<Lib::HAL::DriverMode T_MODE>
struct Lib::BSP::RFID::NfcManager< T_MODE >::Diagnostics

Debug counters and the register evidence captured on the discovery path.

Meant to be inspected with a debugger; the register snapshots are overwritten on every attempt.
