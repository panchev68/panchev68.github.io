Lib::Digi::API::Service::SpectrumAnalyzer

Description

SpectrumAnalyzer service class.

Manages spectrum analysis for Digi XBee modules by performing energy detection scans. Collects and processes spectrum data into current, average, and maximum arrays. Integrates with ServiceBase for lifecycle management and RTOS compatibility.
