Lib::GUI::Position

Description

Point on the display in pixels, counted from the top-left corner: x to the right and y downwards.

A position may lie outside the display; drawing clips it. Positions add and subtract component-wise, so an offset can be applied to a whole group of widgets by changing one base position.
