Lib::BSP::Thermometer::DTH::Base::TimerHandle

Description

Timer handle structure.

Encapsulates a TIM_HandleTypeDef pointer and associated channel for timer operations. Used for configuring timer interrupts and DMA transfers in DTH communication.
