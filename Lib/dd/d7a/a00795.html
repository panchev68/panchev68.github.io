Lib::BSP::DeviceManager

Description

Singleton registry that drives periodic tick events for every registered BSP device.

Devices register and unregister themselves automatically through DeviceBase; setup() calls every registered device's own setup() once and then starts the single shared FreeRTOS timer that dispatches handleTickEvent() to all of them at regular intervals.
