Description

USB OTG port with a run-time role.

Combines a 'USBDevicePort' and a 'USBHostPort' over the same controller. The ST HAL has one handle per role ('PCD_HandleTypeDef' and 'HCD_HandleTypeDef', both with the same 'Instance'), and each role initializes the controller in its own mode, so a switch deinitializes the old role and initializes the new one. The pin that tells the role (the ID pin of the connector) is read by the caller and passed to roleFromIdPin().

Only the active role is initialized. After setRole() the caller configures the role through getDevice() or getHost() (FIFO sizes, event handler, endpoints or channels) and calls its start().

Examples
Lib::HAL::USBDevicePort.

inlinenodiscardnoexcept

Switches the controller to a role.

Stops and deinitializes the active role, then initializes the new one. The bus stays silent after the switch; the caller configures the role and starts it. Task context only.

Parameters
