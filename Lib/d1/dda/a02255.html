Lib::HAL::USBDevicePortBase< T_DERIVED >

Description

template<typename T_DERIVED>
class Lib::HAL::USBDevicePortBase< T_DERIVED >

Compile-time polymorphic foundation implementing the lifecycle and endpoint policy of the USB device port.

Owns no state: the native "PCD_HandleTypeDef" is reached through "derived()->getHandle()", as in "UARTPortBase". The class is a thin typed layer over the ST HAL PCD calls; the clock, pins and the interrupt ("HAL_PCD_MspInit()" and the "OTG_FS_IRQHandler()" calling "HAL_PCD_IRQHandler()") stay in the CubeMX code.
