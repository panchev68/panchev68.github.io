Description

RAII guard that blocks deep sleep for its lifetime, e.g.

while a peripheral needing its clock is busy.

Guards nest and may be held by several drivers at once: deep sleep is allowed again only once every guard has been released. Task context only; an ISR uses acquireDeepSleepLock() and releaseDeepSleepLock().

Examples
RTOS::PowerManager.
