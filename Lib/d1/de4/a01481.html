Lib::Crypto::WolfSSLDhfinal

Description

Finite-field Diffie-Hellman key agreement on wolfCrypt (CubeMX: DH (Diffie-Hellman) Support).

The caller supplies the group through open(), normally a standard group such as an RFC 3526 or RFC 7919 one, since a safe group cannot be chosen on the application's behalf. The key pair lives in caller buffers, not in this instance.

Important
Not thread-safe: give each task its own instance, or guard a shared one with an 'RTOS::Mutex'.
