Lib::Helper::TimeZone::TimeChangeRulefinal

Rule describing a daylight saving time change, such as last Sunday of March at 02:00, UTC+120 min. More...

Description

Rule describing a daylight saving time change, such as last Sunday of March at 02:00, UTC+120 min.

The fields give the week of month, weekday, month and local hour when the change occurs, and the offset from UTC that applies after it.
