Description

Declares Lib::System::SerialNumber, the device serial number derived from the factory-programmed unique identifier.

Hashes the three words of the device unique identifier (UID) with Lib::Helper::CityHash into a 32-bit serial number, available both as a number and as a formatted XXXX-XXXX string.
