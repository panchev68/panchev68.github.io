Lib::Math::Pid< T >::Parameters

Description

template<typename T = float>
struct Lib::Math::Pid< T >::Parameters

Parameters controlling PID behaviour.

Includes gains, sample time and output limits. The default constructor initializes to a proportional-only controller with a sample time of 0.1 seconds and output limits of [-100, 100]. The parameterized constructor allows setting all fields explicitly, with assertions to validate sample time and limits. The setParameters() method can be used to update all parameters at runtime, while setGains(), setOutputLimits() and setSampleTime() allow updating specific aspects of the configuration without affecting other fields.

Construct parameters with explicit values.

Validates that sampleTime is greater than zero and that outputMin is less than or equal to outputMax. These assertions help catch configuration errors early, as a non-positive sample time would lead to division by zero in the derivative term, and invalid output limits could cause unexpected behavior in the controller output. When updating parameters at runtime, care should be taken to ensure that these conditions are met to maintain controller stability and correctness. For example, if the sample time is changed to a smaller value, the integral and derivative terms will respond more quickly, which may require adjusting the gains accordingly.
