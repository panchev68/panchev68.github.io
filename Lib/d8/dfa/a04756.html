Lib::Canbus::ServiceBase::RxMessage

Minimal classic-CAN received-message wrapper exposed to derived services. More...

Description

Minimal classic-CAN received-message wrapper exposed to derived services.

Decouples derived services from the HAL frame layout; built from a Lib::HAL::CANFrame as read off Lib::HAL::CANPort::read().
