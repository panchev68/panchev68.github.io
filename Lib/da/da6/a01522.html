Lib::Helper::Convert::Hex< uint16_t >

Description

Hex specialization for uint16_t.

Provides conversion methods for 16-bit unsigned integers to hexadecimal strings and BCD representations. This specialization handles two-byte values.

Role within the module: Offers type-safe conversions for uint16_t values.

Important design decisions: Implements BCD conversion algorithmically for efficiency.
