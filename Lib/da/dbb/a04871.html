Lib::Crypto::WolfSSLRsafinal

Description

RSA key generation, PKCS#1 v1.5 signatures and encryption on wolfCrypt (CubeMX: RSA Support).

generateKey() also attaches the generator to the key, since wolfCrypt's RSA blinding needs it for private key operations. Signatures and encrypted blocks are exactly encryptedSize() bytes long.

Important
sign() pads the given bytes as they are, without the DigestInfo header: for interoperability with other PKCS#1 v1.5 verifiers, pass the DER DigestInfo of the hash, not the bare hash.
The WolfSSLRandom passed to generateKey() must outlive the key, or be replaced by a later generateKey() call.
Not thread-safe: give each task its own instance, or guard a shared one with an RTOS::Mutex.
