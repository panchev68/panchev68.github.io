Lib::Digi::API::Service::ChannelScannerfinal

Description

Moves the link to every channel through "Parameters" and records the quality measured there.

One scan starts on every "open()" and runs in the application task, in the same task as "Parameters", so the channel changes need no locking. A channel is left after the connection quality reports a full scanner-mode window or after "SCAN_CHANNEL_PERIOD". The channels are the 2.4 GHz range 0x0B to 0x1A written to "CH". When the scan ends the service stays running, idle, until "close()".
