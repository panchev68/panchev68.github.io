requires (N_BITS > 0U && N_BITS <= std::numeric_limits<uint32_t>::digits)
class Lib::Helper::BitSet< N_BITS >

Fixed-width event bitmask with per-bit access, stored in a 'uint32_t'.

Bits at or above 'N_BITS' are always kept cleared. No destructor is declared on purpose: a user-declared one, even defaulted, makes the class non-literal on this toolchain and prevents 'constexpr' instances.

Template Parameters
N_BITSNumber of managed bits (1..32).
