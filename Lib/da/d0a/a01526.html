Lib::Helper::Convert::Hex< uint32_t >

Description

Hex specialization for uint32_t.

Provides conversion methods for 32-bit unsigned integers to hexadecimal strings and BCD representations. This specialization handles four-byte values.

Role within the module: Offers type-safe conversions for uint32_t values.

Important design decisions: Implements BCD conversion algorithmically for efficiency.
