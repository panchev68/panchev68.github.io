Lib::GUI::Context::ViewportScopefinal

Description

Limits drawing to a part of the display for one scope and gives the previous area back when the scope ends.

The rectangle is counted in the current area and cut to it, so scopes nest. Inside the scope every coordinate starts at the top-left corner of the rectangle and the widgets, the text and the shapes are clipped to it.
