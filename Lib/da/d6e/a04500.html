template<Lib::HAL::DriverMode T_MODE>
struct Lib::BSP::RFID::ST25R100< T_MODE >::TxRxDiagnostics

Snapshot of the transmit/receive pipeline state.

Meant to be captured right after a failed REQA/anticollision exchange so a debugger can see hard register evidence instead of guessing at the cause.
