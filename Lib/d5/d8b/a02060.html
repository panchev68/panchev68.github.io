Lib::Digi::Core::LocalAtCommand

Description

Facilitates sending local AT commands to a Module and receiving their responses.

The class registers a callback on the Module to capture Frame0x88 responses, stores them in an RTOS queue and exposes simple blocking receive/send APIs.
