RTOS::StreamBufferfinal

Description

Owner of a FreeRTOS stream buffer handle.

FreeRTOS stream buffers are lock-free only for a single writer and a single reader: two tasks blocked in send(), or two blocked in receive(), trip a kernel assertion. Several writers or readers must serialize their calls themselves, e.g. with a RTOS::Mutex, and then use a zero timeout. A reader blocked in receive() wakes up once at least the trigger level is buffered or its timeout expires, and then returns whatever is available.
