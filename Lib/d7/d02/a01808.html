Lib::HAL::ADCPort< DriverMode::BLOCKING >final

Description

Explicit specialized variant driving the blocking start/poll/stop ADC single-conversion path.

A full blocking single-shot read is "start()", then "poll(timeout)", then "stop()". This matches how a blocking regular-group conversion works on this hardware: "HAL_ADC_PollForConversion()" spins on the end-of-conversion flag itself rather than waiting on an interrupt. Does not depend on DeviceManager or Device, since a blocking conversion completes inside the poll call and raises no HAL callback to route.
