Lib::BSP::DigitalPeripheralBase::Outputsfinal

Description

Output port driving a daisy chain of MC33879 low-side switches.

Every frame starts with a sequence byte that travels through the whole chain and must come back as the last received byte; a mismatch means a broken chain. Drivers are enabled only after the first successful frame, so they never switch on with undefined data.
