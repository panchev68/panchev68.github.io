Lib::GUI::Primitivesfinal

Description

Pixels, lines and rectangles with optional rounded corners on one display, shared by the other GUI classes.

Binds the Lib::GUI::Display once through its configuration; Lib::GUI::Canvas and every widget above it draw with the same instance. All shapes are built from Lib::GUI::Display::setPixel() and are clipped by the display. Everything goes into the frame buffer of the driver, nothing is sent to the panel before Lib::GUI::Primitives::flush().

A line is walked with the Bresenham algorithm; every lit step is a square of the line thickness centered on the line, and the pattern steps are as long as the thickness, so a thick dashed line keeps its proportions. Rectangles are given by their top-left Lib::GUI::Position and their size, and the rounded ones by the radius of the corner arcs, which is limited to half of the shorter side; the arcs are drawn with the midpoint circle algorithm.
