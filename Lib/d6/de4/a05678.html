Lib::HAL::WindowWatchdogfinal

Description

Controls the hardware Window Watchdog peripheral subsystem.

Encapsulates the native STM32 HAL watchdog initialization and counter refresh sequences to protect the firmware against execution deviations by enforcing strict window-bound refresh timings.
