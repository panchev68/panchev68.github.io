Description

Resistive touch screen controller driver for the XPT2046.

Interrupt-driven and thread-safe. A bound NIRQ interrupt wakes an internal RTOS task, which samples the controller over bit-banged SPI, filters the readings through integration and maps them to calibrated display coordinates. Readers get the latest position through read().

XPT2046 datasheet (Waveshare copy), third-party; the original is by Shenzhen XPTEK
