Lib::Helper::Sunset Class Referencefinal

Description

Results are expressed in minutes since local midnight of the set date. A value below 0 or at or above 1440 means that the event falls on the previous or next local day. During polar day or polar night the hour angle is clamped, so sunrise and sunset collapse to solar midnight or solar noon respectively.
