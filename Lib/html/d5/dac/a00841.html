Description

Cryptographic primitives and optional TLS/SSH client wrappers.

Provides a hardware-accelerated SHA-256 wrapper over the STM32 HASH peripheral, plus "WolfSSL" and "WolfSSH" wrappers over the optional I-CUBE-wolfSSL/I-CUBE-wolfSSH middleware packs, each compiling to nothing when its pack is not generated into the project.
