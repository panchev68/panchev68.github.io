inlinenodiscardconstexprnoexcept

The local time is shifted by this zone's offset to UTC, expressed in seconds since 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC, reduced by offsetSeconds and floored to whole minutes.

Parameters
yearYear, 1970 or later.
monthMonth in range [1,12].
dayDay of month, valid for the given month and year.
hourHour of day in range [0,23].
minuteMinute in range [0,59].
offsetSecondsEpoch offset in seconds subtracted before converting to minutes.
Returns
UTC minutes since the epoch, or INVALID_MINUTES on invalid input or overflow.
