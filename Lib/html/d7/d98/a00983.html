std::optional< float > Lib::BSP::Thermometer::OneWire::DS18B20::readTemperatureCelsius ( )
inlinenodiscard

Issues a bus reset, a Skip ROM, a Convert T, waits out the worst-case 12-bit conversion time, then reads back and CRC-validates the scratchpad.

Returns
Measured temperature in degrees Celsius, or 'std::nullopt' on a missing device or a failed CRC.
