Lib::GUI::Primitivesfinal

Description

Pixels, lines, rectangles, circles, ellipses, arcs, pie slices, triangles and curves on one display, shared by the other GUI classes.

Binds the Lib::GUI::Display once through its configuration; Lib::GUI::Canvas and every widget above it draw with the same instance. All shapes are built from Lib::GUI::Display::setPixel() and are clipped by the display. Everything goes into the frame buffer of the driver, nothing is sent to the panel before Lib::GUI::Primitives::flush().

A line is walked with the Bresenham algorithm; every lit step is a square of the line thickness centered on the line, and the pattern steps are as long as the thickness, so a thick dashed line keeps its proportions. Rectangles are given by their top-left Lib::GUI::Position and their size, and the rounded ones by the radius of the corner arcs, which is limited to half of the shorter side; the arcs are drawn with the midpoint circle algorithm.

Every angle is in degrees, counted counter-clockwise from the right, as on a drawing: 0 points right, 90 up, 180 left and 270 down. An arc runs from its start angle to its end angle in that direction; an end angle that is not larger than the start angle goes round through 360, and an end angle exactly 360 above the start angle makes a full circle.

Draws a quadratic Bezier curve.

The curve starts at the first point, is pulled towards the control point and ends at the last point. It is drawn as short connected lines, as many as the length of its control polygon in pixels, so it has no gaps.

Parameters
startFirst point of the curve.
controlControl point; the curve does not pass through it.
endLast point of the curve.
isOntrue to light the pixels, false to clear them.

Draws the outline of an ellipse or of an elliptical arc.

A whole ellipse is drawn row by row and column by column, so the outline has no gaps. An arc is drawn as short lines one degree apart.

Parameters
centerCenter of the ellipse.
xRadiusHorizontal radius in pixels; nothing is drawn if both radii are 0 or one is negative.
yRadiusVertical radius in pixels.
isOntrue to light the pixels, false to clear them.
startAngleAngle where the arc starts, in degrees.
endAngleAngle where the arc ends, in degrees; the default pair gives the whole ellipse.

◆ fillRectRaster()

void Lib::GUI::Primitives::fillRectRaster ( const Position position,
const int32_t width,
const int32_t height )
inlinenoexcept

Draws a filled rectangle with a checkerboard of lit and dark pixels, a 50% grey.

The pattern is the same for every rectangle: the pixels where the sum of the column and the row is even are lit and the others are dark, so neighbouring rectangles and a pattern drawn twice line up.

Parameters
positionTop-left corner.
widthWidth in pixels; nothing is drawn for 0 or less.
heightHeight in pixels; nothing is drawn for 0 or less.
