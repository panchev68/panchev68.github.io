Lib::BSP::Backlight

Display backlight intensity driven by a PWM timer channel. More...

Description

Display backlight intensity driven by a PWM timer channel.

The output fades one compare unit per fade step towards the target brightness and drops to the minimum after the configured period without user activity. Right after construction it fades up from dark to the maximum.
