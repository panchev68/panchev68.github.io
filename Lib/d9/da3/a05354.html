Lib::HAL::CANPort< DriverMode::BLOCKING >final

Description

Blocking specialization: polls for mailbox or FIFO readiness instead of using the interrupt notification of CANPort.

Does not depend on DeviceManager or Device, since no HAL callback is ever registered.
