RTOS::Mutex

Description

Mutex provides ownership of a FreeRTOS semaphore handle and exposes convenience helpers for locking/unlocking from task context.

The wrapper intentionally keeps a small surface area: open/close manage the underlying handle, and lock/unlock perform take/give operations. Recursive mutex behavior is selected at construction.
