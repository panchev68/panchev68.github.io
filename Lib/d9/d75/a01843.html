Lib::Ethernet::Wiznet::Socketfinal

Description

One hardware socket of the W5500, for TCP and UDP communication.

Implements the socket operations (open, connect, listen, send, receive and the datagram pair) directly on the chip registers through "W5500", following the socket sequences in the W5500 datasheet. Buffers are "std::span", addresses and ports are the "Node" type of this library. All calls block the calling task, polling the chip with short delays, and give up after a timeout.

Instances are created only by "Sockets", which maps each object to its hardware socket number.
