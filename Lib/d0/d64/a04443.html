Lib::BSP::DeviceManager

Description

Singleton registry that drives periodic tick events for every registered BSP device.

Devices register and unregister themselves automatically through DeviceBase. setup() calls every registered device's own setup() once and then starts the single shared FreeRTOS timer that dispatches DeviceBase::handleTickEvent() to all of them at regular intervals.
