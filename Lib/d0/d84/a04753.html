Description

ECDSA signatures and ECDHE key agreement on the SECP256R1 curve, on wolfCrypt (CubeMX: ECC Support).

A key is created with generateKey(), which also attaches the generator to the key: wolfCrypt's timing resistant ECDHE needs it for sharedSecret(). Signatures are DER encoded and cover a caller-supplied digest.

Important
The WolfSSLRandom passed to generateKey() must outlive the key, or be replaced by a later generateKey() call.
There is no import or export of public keys yet: verify() and sharedSecret() work only with keys generated in this program.
Not thread-safe: give each task its own instance, or guard a shared one with an RTOS::Mutex.
Examples
Lib::Crypto::WolfSSL.
