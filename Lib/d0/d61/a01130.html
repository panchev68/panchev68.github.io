Description

Transport-agnostic MQTT 3.1.1 client on wolfMQTT.

Lib::MQTT::Client connects, publishes, subscribes and receives over any caller-supplied byte-stream transport satisfying the Lib::MQTT::MqttTransport concept. The header compiles to nothing without the wolfMQTT middleware.

Enumerator

BAD_ARGUMENT 

An argument was null, empty or out of range.

NOT_CONNECTED 

The operation needs an established connection.

BUFFER_TOO_SMALL 

The packet did not fit into the transmit or receive buffer.

NETWORK_ERROR 

The transport failed to connect, send or receive.

TIMEOUT 

Nothing arrived or completed in time; for Client::process() this is the normal idle result.

TLS_ERROR 

The TLS handshake failed.

REFUSED 

The broker refused the connection or the subscription.

PROTOCOL_ERROR 

The broker sent a malformed or unexpected packet.

FAILURE 

Any other wolfMQTT error, or the client lock could not be taken.
