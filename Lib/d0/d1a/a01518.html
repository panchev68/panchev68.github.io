Lib::Helper::Convert::Hex< uint8_t >

Description

Hex specialization for uint8_t.

Provides conversion methods for 8-bit unsigned integers to hexadecimal strings and BCD representations. This specialization handles single-byte values.

Role within the module: Offers type-safe conversions for uint8_t values.

Important design decisions: Uses HexHelper for low-level operations to ensure consistency.
